Social risk factors for the development of mental illness or mental health deterioration can include:

  • Lack of social support and/or isolation from others
  • Abusive relationships
  • Weak social safety net (family and community factors)
  • Low socioeconomic status
  • Exposure to discrimination

Biological risk factors for the development of mental illness or mental health deterioration can include:

  • Genetic vulnerability
  • Health comorbidities (or generally poor physical health)
  • Poor nutrition or sleep patterns
  • Neurological deficits or injuries
  • Substance abuse

Psychological risk factors for the development of mental illness or mental health deterioration can include:

  • Prolonged exposure to stress
  • Traumatic experience(s)
  • Dissatisfaction with current life status
  • Self-depreciation
  • Certain personality traits, thoughts, emotions, and attitudes

3.3 Section Summary

  • Social, biological, and psychological factors can increase our risk of developing a mental illness or experiencing mental health deterioration.
  • Some of these factors may be out of our control (i.e., genetic vulnerabilities), while others are very much within our control (i.e., nutrition, physical activity, and sleep quality).
  • Our physical and mental health are closely related - taking care of ourselves physically can help us to maintain good mental health.
